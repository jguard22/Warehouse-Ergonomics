# Design and UX review: Warehouse Ergonomics demo (25 Oct 2026)

**Art direction.** A shift report you can read across a loading dock: calm and instrumented, not a game. Ink-navy ground and brand teal, a Müller-Brockmann grid with hairline rules in place of cards, Vignelli-scale numerals, Tufte direct labelling (the zone thresholds are named on the chart, nothing sits in a legend). **Signature: the shift ribbon**, a trunk-flexion trace across the shift that stamps every lift on its peak, ring for good form and diamond for risky, and that doubles as the scrubber on a recording.

Audience: enterprise and safety buyers watching a demo on a laptop or a TV from 2 to 3 m. Primary action: **Start shift**. Scope: design, animation and UX only. No detection maths, thresholds or safety rules were changed (a unit test pins them).

## Baseline (before)

- Tests: none. Smoke: none. Console: clean. Sideways overflow: **yes**, at 390 px (the gauge readouts ran to 396) and at 320 px (376 to 396; the gallery 324).

## Not changed, and why

- **Dark only.** The screen is for a TV or a meeting-room laptop and the brief is navy and teal; there is no light theme. The canvases and the 3D scene would need a second token set to follow one.
- **No unit switch.** The page shows degrees, percent, seconds and counts only; no length, weight or speed. I did not add a map scale bar, which would imply a precision the stride estimate does not have.
- **"Apps must be fun".** This is a calm enterprise demo (per the brief), so there is no XP or streak layer; the juice is the micro-motion above.
- **Good lift in the red zone.** The simulated good lift peaks at 53 degrees, so the log shows a red-zone cue just before "good form". That is the pipeline's own definition (flexion zone for the buzz, hip hinge for the score); left as is.
- **Simulator pacing.** The first risky lift lands 17 to 19 s into the shift (walk, good lift, walk, stooped lift). Left as is.
- **Fonts, three.js and the SDK stub load from CDNs.** Offline, the type falls back to system fonts and the 3D panel stays empty. Self-hosting means committing font files; not done.
- Detection, the vision foot-track pipeline, the SDK adapter (dormant) and the recordings themselves are untouched.

## Added display quantities (need Jeff's OK)

- **Time on feet**: seconds where combined insole load is at least 0.15 (each side 0 to 1), gaps over 250 ms ignored. It is a display tally, not a detection threshold.
- **Load asymmetry**: |L - R| / (L + R) of insole load integrated over time on feet, shown after 3 s. It replaces the old step-count "L/R asymmetry", which is no longer displayed.
